Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions parents ask when considering admissions at Restart Classes.

Q. Which classes, boards and mediums are covered?

We exclusively specialize in English Medium for students from Classes 4 to 10 across CBSE and GSEB boards for all school subjects.

Q. What is the student-teacher ratio in the class?

We maintain a maximum of 6–8 students per teacher so every student receives individual attention, a customized learning pace, and direct doubt-clearing from the teacher.

Q. Do you offer demo or trial classes?

Yes! We encourage students to attend a free trial class to experience our interactive, concept-first teaching method before making a decision.

Q. How are parents updated about progress?

We conduct regular tests and counselling sessions with students, and share progress updates with parents every month via call or WhatsApp.
